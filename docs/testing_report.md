## What the results establish and their limits

The required data, KPI, statistical and forecasting areas already have test/validation coverage; the unchanged current suite passes, and the actual source files retain their Phase 1 bytes. Independent earlier computations support the saved full-data results, and notebook artifacts retain successful execution records.

This is not exhaustive path coverage, a fresh end-to-end analytical/model rerun, proof of universal skill activation, validation of unseen business conditions, or an achieved waste/stockout benefit. Fixture tests and historical output checks cannot establish causality, exact safety stock, future forecast performance or test MAE without observed targets. Future phases require their own authorized checks. **Phase 11 is complete; Phase 12 remains unstarted.**
